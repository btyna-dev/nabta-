# Nabta 🌱

AI-powered personalized learning planner.

## 💡 About the Project

Nabta is a simple and beginner-friendly study planner designed to help students create a personalized study plan based on their subject, available study time, study level, and learning goal.

The idea is to make studying more organized, simple, and motivating.

## ✨ Features

- 📚 Choose the subject you want to study
- ⏰ Select your available study time
- 📈 Choose your study level
- 🎯 Set your learning goal
- 🌱 Generate a personalized study plan
- ✅ Check off completed tasks
- 📊 Track your daily progress
- 💾 Save progress in the browser
- 🧹 Reset your progress anytime

## 🛠️ Technologies

- HTML
- CSS
- JavaScript
- Local Storage

## 🎯 Target Users

Nabta is designed especially for students and beginner learners who want a simple way to organize their study time and track their progress.

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Open Nabta.
2. Enter the subject you want to study.
3. Choose your study time.
4. Select your study level.
5. Enter your learning goal.
6. Click **"أنشئ خطتي ✨"**.
7. Follow the generated study plan and check tasks as you complete them.

## 🌱 Why Nabta?

Nabta aims to make learning feel less overwhelming by turning a study goal into small, clear, and achievable steps.
